## ThreeUI Community (MIT)

`js/core3d.js` adapts two shaders from ThreeUI Community
(https://github.com/MengTo/threeui):

- **Liquid Form**: the layered simplex-noise displacement stack (`lqSnoise`, `lqDisp`),
  moved from a ray-marched fragment shader into a Babylon.js PBR material plugin.
- **Ribbon Field**: the ribbon + halftone dot-matrix background, recoloured per style.
